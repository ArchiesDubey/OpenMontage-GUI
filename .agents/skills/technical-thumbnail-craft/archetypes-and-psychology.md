## The 120px Mobile Scale Test

Over 70% of YouTube impressions occur on mobile screens, where thumbnails render at approximately **120px to 160px in height**.

### Mobile Validation Checklist:
1. **The Squint Test**: Scale down the thumbnail to 10% of its size. Can you still read the 2–3 words of headline text instantly?
2. **The 3-Element Limit**: Count the distinct visual chunks. If there are more than 3 (e.g. Headline, Left Object, Right Object), remove elements until only 3 remain.
3. **No Small Body Text**: Never include paragraphs, complex bullet points, or tiny labels. Any secondary technical tag (e.g., `MINIMUM 1 TB` or `8GB`) must be bold, short, and enclosed in a high-contrast pill badge.
4. **Bottom-Right Exclusion Zone**: YouTube overlays the video duration stamp (e.g., `14:22`) in the bottom-right corner. Keep the bottom-right $220\text{px} \times 90\text{px}$ completely free of crucial graphics or text.
